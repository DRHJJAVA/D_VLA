# Sampling-density and contact-coverage audit (2026-10-23)

All calculations ran on the remote server. No image, point cloud, HDF5, checkpoint, or contact-coordinate file was transferred locally. Contact deduplication preserves the original coordinates (`src/evaluate_contact_coverage.py`, commit b81be05); earlier coverage values computed after snapping coordinates to a 3 mm grid are superseded.

The 4,096-point held-out run used 33/7/7 episodes and 132/28/28 train/validation/test frames. Its best validation-BCE checkpoint is epoch 3 of 8. Validation mean AP on points with soft target >0.5 is 0.5619; test mean AP is 0.4672. The test AP applies to 17 of 28 frames with such points and is not a physical grasp-success metric. All labels remain provisional pending visual review.

The evaluator transforms each held-out event's object-frame contacts to the pre-contact observation pose. It marks a contact visible when either camera's depth agrees within max(1 cm, 2 sigma). It ranks offline target-object points but never inputs object IDs to the model. Coverage is averaged first across frames in an episode and then across episodes; a sample with no visible contacts is excluded from the coverage denominator but remains in visibility statistics. The random baseline draws an equal-size object-point set 100 times per frame.

**Validation-only distance audit before the 16k model result:** Of the candidate contact positions, 44.5% pass the two-camera depth visibility check in these validation frames. With 4,096 scene points the median target-object count is 55.5. The median nearest sampled target point to a visible contact is 9.39 mm (p90 16.72 mm), and contact-weighted sampling ceilings are 3.5% at the sample's sigma and 54.4% at 10 mm. With 16,384 scene points the median target-object count rises to 215.5; nearest distance median is 5.11 mm (p90 10.35 mm), and ceilings rise to 33.3% at sigma and 87.7% at 10 mm. The same four training frames used for the initial 16k technical pilot had mean target points 55.25→223 and high-label points 5.5→27; graph construction and a training backward pass worked.

**Evaluation choice frozen before 16k model inspection:** Use 10 mm as the primary contact-distance tolerance for 10%, 20%, and 30% object-point budgets, with sigma-sized tolerance as a sensitivity analysis. The 10 mm value is close to the validation p90 nearest-point distance after 16k sampling and retains an 87.7% sampling ceiling. Report visibility and the all-point sampling ceiling alongside model and equal-budget random coverage. This is a point-distance metric on sampled visible surfaces, not a claim that any selected point yields a physically feasible gripper pose. The initial 4k test ceiling was examined before this decision as a density diagnostic and must be disclosed in the final analysis; no 16k test result was used to choose the tolerance.

The 16k pipeline is isolated under `data/samples/single_frame_16k` and `outputs/alphabet_soup_pointnet2_16k_provisional`, and waits for shared GPU utilization <=70% with >=20 GB free on two checks 30 seconds apart before training. Next gates: visual review of 30 contact events and RGB-D surface alignment (at least 27 correct), three-model comparison under the same split, and an unseen-asset split. This task contains one known asset only and cannot support an unseen-asset generalization claim.
