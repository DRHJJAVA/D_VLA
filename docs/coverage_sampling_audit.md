# Sampling-density and contact-coverage audit (2026-10-23)

All calculations were executed on the remote server. No image, point cloud, HDF5, checkpoint, or contact-coordinate file was transferred locally.

The 4,096-point held-out run used 33/7/7 episodes and 132/28/28 train/validation/test frames. Its best validation-BCE checkpoint is epoch 3 of 8. Validation mean AP on points with soft target >0.5 is 0.5619, and test mean AP is 0.4672. The test AP applies to 17 of 28 frames with such points; it is not a grasp-success metric. All labels remain provisional pending visual review.

`src/evaluate_contact_coverage.py` transforms each held-out event's object-frame contacts to the pre-contact observation pose. It marks a contact visible when either camera's depth agrees within max(1 cm, 2 sigma), then measures distance to the top 10%, 20%, or 30% of offline target-object sample points. Object IDs are used only for evaluation, never as model input. Results are averaged first across frames of each episode and then across episodes. Samples with no visible contacts are excluded from coverage but counted in visibility reporting. The random baseline draws equal-size target-object point sets 100 times per sample.

At the plan's initial sigma-sized distance tolerance, validation mean visible-contact fraction is 0.475; 24/28 frames have any visible contact. Validation sampling ceiling (all target points) is only 0.0298 after episode aggregation. Test mean visible-contact fraction is 0.619; 25/28 frames have visible contacts, with sampling ceiling 0.0685. Thus current contact-coverage values largely measure sparse sampling. Validation target-object point count ranges 23–80, median 55.5. Across 59 visible validation contacts, the nearest target-point distance has p10 4.50 mm, median 9.17 mm, p90 17.0 mm. Contact-weighted ceilings are 0.051 at sigma, 0.153 at 5 mm, 0.576 at 10 mm and 0.949 at 20 mm. These are diagnostic sensitivity figures, not a test-tuned metric.

On the same four training frames from demo 26, the 16,384-point pilot increased mean target points 55.25→223 and mean high-label points 5.5→27. A 16k graph and one training backward pass completed. The full 16k experiment is isolated from the 4k files and waits for shared GPU utilization <=70% with >=20 GB free on two checks 30 seconds apart. After it finishes, choose any revised coverage tolerance on validation only, freeze it, then calculate test coverage. The initial 4k test ceiling has already been inspected for diagnosis and must be disclosed if a revised metric is reported.
